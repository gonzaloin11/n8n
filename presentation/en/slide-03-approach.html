EXPENSE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM WITH AI

30 - 01 - 2026

The Approach

I didn't build "a new app." I adapted to the existing way of working and transformed it: the client does what they always do (send a message) and everything else is automated behind the scenes.

Core idea

Keep the user's behavior intact: if they can send a message (like WhatsApp), they can use the system.

Single entry point via Telegram: photos, PDFs, audio or text. No learning curve, no friction.

What happens behind

  • An AI agent understands, structures and classifies the information.

  • Expenses "find their place" automatically: fewer errors, more control.

  • If something doesn't add up, the system detects it, requests clarification or flags it for review.

The client only sees the result: everything flows.
